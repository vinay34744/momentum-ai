Privacy Policy & Local Data Statement

Last updated: September 21, 2026 • Version 1.0.0 (Phase 0)

Notice & Product Information Disclaimer
This document provides accurate product information regarding data storage in Momentum AI Version 1. It is provided for informational purposes and does not constitute formal legal advice.

1. Local-First Architecture

Momentum AI Version 1 is designed as a local-first application. All data created within the application—including user profile information, goals, tasks, study logs, health entries, and settings—is stored directly in your browser's LocalStorage.

Your data remains on your device. Momentum AI does not transmit your personal records, execution metrics, or notes to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party tracking services in Version 1.

2. Explicit Scope of Security & Storage

To ensure complete transparency:

  • No Cloud Storage: Data is not synced to AWS, Google Cloud, or remote servers.
  • No Server-Side Encryption: Storage relies on your browser and operating system's native local storage mechanisms.
  • No Compliance Certifications: Version 1 does not claim formal GDPR, HIPAA, or ISO security compliance certifications.
  • User Control: You can inspect, export, or permanently erase your stored state at any time via the System State Inspector interface.

3. LocalStorage & Browser Persistence

Data stored in LocalStorage persists across browser sessions. However, clearing your browser cache, using private/incognito windows, or clearing site storage will remove your local data unless you have previously exported a JSON backup file.

4. Future Versions

If future phases introduce optional cloud backup, synchronization, or AI features, explicit opt-in consent and updated privacy documentation will be provided prior to any data transmission.
